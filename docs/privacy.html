CPS Supervision

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 24, 2025

Overview

Your data never leaves your device or your personal iCloud account. We do not collect, sell, or share your information with any third party.

CPS Supervision ("the App") is a supervision log tool for individuals involved in child welfare supervision programs. This policy explains what information the App stores, where it is stored, and how it is protected.

Information You Enter

The App stores only the information you choose to enter, which may include:

  • Child profiles — name, date of birth, case number, case worker contact
  • Supervisor profiles — name, relationship, contact information
  • Supervision session logs — dates, times, locations, activities, notes
  • Incident reports — type, description, severity, actions taken
  • Photos optionally attached to child profiles

Where Your Data Is Stored

On your device. All data is stored locally using Apple's Core Data framework and never transmitted to our servers — because we do not operate any servers.

In your iCloud account (optional). If you use iCloud sync or share access with co-supervisors, data is synced through Apple's CloudKit service using your personal iCloud account. This data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy and is encrypted in transit and at rest.

We never see your data. We have no access to any information you enter into the App.

Data Sharing

Your data is never shared with advertisers, analytics services, or any third party by us.

The only sharing that occurs is when you choose to share access with a co-supervisor using the App's built-in iCloud sharing feature. This sharing is entirely under your control and uses Apple's secure CloudKit infrastructure.

Data Security

  • All local data is protected by iOS device encryption
  • The App supports Face ID and Touch ID to prevent unauthorized access
  • iCloud sync uses end-to-end encryption provided by Apple
  • We do not maintain any external databases, servers, or backups

Children's Privacy

CPS Supervision is intended for use by adults (supervisors, case workers, and foster parents) involved in child welfare supervision programs. The App is not directed at children and is not intended to be used by individuals under 18.

While the App stores information about children in the context of supervision records, this information is entered and controlled entirely by adult users and is stored only on their personal devices and iCloud accounts.

We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

Data Retention & Deletion

You are in full control of your data at all times:

  • Delete individual records at any time within the App
  • Delete all data by uninstalling the App (local data is removed automatically)
  • Remove iCloud data via Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud → Manage Storage on your device

Analytics & Tracking

The App does not use any analytics tools, crash reporting services, advertising SDKs, or tracking technologies. We do not track you across apps or websites.

Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this privacy policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
